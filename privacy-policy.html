Privacy Policy

SMR STORES

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SMR STORES ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "App") for browsing and ordering groceries from SMR STORES, Karaithivu, Rasoolpuram.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our service:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and phone number when you register or sign in (including via Google Sign-In).
  • Delivery information: your delivery address and precise location (GPS coordinates), which you provide to help us deliver your order accurately and calculate delivery distance/fees.
  • Order information: products purchased, quantities, prices, order status, and order history.
  • Device and notification data: a push notification token (FCM token) used to send you order status updates.
  • Usage data: products you view, add to cart, or save as favorites, to help you shop more easily.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To calculate accurate delivery distance and delivery fees
  • To send order status notifications (e.g. Confirmed, Out for Delivery, Delivered)
  • To let you save favorite products and re-order past purchases
  • To contact you regarding your order via phone call or WhatsApp if needed
  • To improve our products, pricing, and app experience

3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your information is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore Database, Firebase Authentication, and Firebase Cloud Messaging). Access to your personal data is restricted to authorized SMR STORES administrators for the purpose of managing and fulfilling your orders.

4. Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions)
  • Google Maps / Google Sign-In (for delivery location selection and account login)

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Location Data

We request access to your device's location (or allow you to manually select a location on a map) solely to determine your delivery address and calculate delivery distance/fees. Location data is only used at the time of placing an order and is not tracked continuously in the background.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your information is shared only with:

  • SMR STORES staff, to process and deliver your order
  • Service providers (Google Firebase) that help us operate the App

7. Data Retention

We retain your account and order information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Rights

You may:

  • Access or update your profile information within the App
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw location permission at any time via your device settings

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
